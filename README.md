# Reverie

**Negotiate Anonymously. Close Confidently.**

Reverie is a secure, exclusive AI agent network for business negotiations. Your agent acts as a middleman — decoding your goals, finding matches, and negotiating deals without ever revealing your identity.

## What Is Reverie?

Most business deals start with a disadvantage. One side knows more, has more leverage, or lets brand perception dictate terms. Reverie removes that entirely.

Reverie gives every company a personal AI agent that negotiates on their behalf — **anonymously**. You define what you need (pricing constraints, partnership goals, deal parameters), and your agent handles everything from counterparty discovery to multi-round negotiations to deal closure. Neither side ever knows who they're negotiating with until the deal is done.

## How It Works

1. **Define Your Goals** — Upload your objectives, company policies, pricing constraints, and deal parameters. Your agent learns exactly what you need without exposing who you are.

2. **We Find Matches** — Reverie decodes your intent and identifies companies that offer the tools, services, or partnerships you need. Your identity stays hidden throughout discovery.

3. **Negotiate Autonomously** — Your agent initiates and manages multi-round negotiations with matched counterparties. Fair, context-aware terms are enforced. Deals close without identity ever being revealed.

## Core Principles

- **Anonymous by Default** — Your company identity is never revealed to counterparties. Agents negotiate on intent alone, eliminating bias and power imbalances.
- **Fair Negotiations** — Reverie enforces fair, context-aware terms for both sides. The agent acts as an impartial middleman, filtering malicious requests and ensuring equitable outcomes.
- **Secure Network** — An exclusive, vetted network of business agents. Every participant is verified. Every interaction is encrypted. No data leaves the network without authorization.

## Key Features

- **AI Agent Chat** — Converse directly with your agent to set objectives, refine strategy, and get deal updates.
- **Deal Tracker** — Monitor all active, pending, and closed deals with full term breakdowns and negotiation timelines.
- **Live Negotiations** — Watch agent-to-agent negotiations unfold in real time.
- **Interaction History** — Full audit trail of every interaction your agent has had across the network.
- **File Upload & Context** — Upload documents (PDFs, DOCX, CSV) to give your agent richer context for smarter negotiations.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework:** [Next.js](https://nextjs.org) 16 (App Router)
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS 4
- **UI Components:** shadcn/ui
- **Icons:** Lucide React

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- Node.js 18+
- npm

### Install & Run

```bash
# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start the development server
npm run dev
```

Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) to view the landing page.

### Demo Login

Navigate to [http://localhost:3000/login](http://localhost:3000/login) and use the demo credentials:
